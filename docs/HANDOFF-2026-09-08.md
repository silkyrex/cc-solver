## The division of labour that is easy to get wrong

The task prompts say it out loud: *"where this prompt and the repo differ, the repo wins."*

- **What the solver OUTPUTS** — repo controls it. Adding a field needs no Notion edit.
- **What the task writes IN** — the Notion prompt alone controls it. Requiring a new input field
  always needs a prompt edit.

Missing that split is exactly how the 12-hour `pt_time` change crashed `take_action` (PR #5).

## Traps worth knowing

- **A test that passes for the wrong reason.** Two attempts at the `confirm_pass` pending-exit test
  were green while testing nothing: an uptrend entry binds to the 21 EMA door, and any close far
  enough below the 4 EMA to matter is already a deep break, i.e. *confirmed* rather than pending.
  Use a relaunch-shaped fixture. The reasoning is in the fixture docstring.
- **A provisional bar must never graduate a position.** Graduation is one-way, so an intraday pop
  over the 21 EMA that fades by the close would loosen the leash permanently. The live bar carries
  `provisional=True` and graduation skips it.
- **Never guess a side.** A wrong side inverts the stop onto the profitable side of price. Both
  `position_monitor` and `confirm_pass` return `SIDE UNKNOWN` and push rather than defaulting.
- **The exclusion matcher still needs both halves.** A leverage word on a word boundary AND an
  issuer token. Compound forms (`UltraShort`, `UltraProShort`) are enumerated explicitly, because
  boundary matching means `Ultra` no longer matches inside `UltraPro`.
